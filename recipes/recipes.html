Pot Roast Recipe

ingredients

  • Beef Chuck Roast
  • Carrots, Celery, Potatoes
  • Mushrooms
  • Onions
  • Tomato Paste
  • Flour
  • Olive oil, or lard
  • Red wine or white wine
  • Thyme/Parsley/Bay Leaves
  • Beef stock, or vegetable stock

Steps

  1. Season beef, place on a sheet tray overnight in refridgerator
  2. Add olive oil to pot, place in beef, sear all sides until golden brown
  3. Set beef aside, add in onions/leeks, saute for 6 minutes<. Turn heat down.
  4. Put in garlic
  5. Deglaze with 1/4 cup wine, cook until absorbed
  6. Stir in tomato paste, cook 2 to 3 minutes
  7. Pout in 3/4 cup wine, beef stock, bay leaves, all herbs
  8. Add back in the seared beef, bring mixture to boil, add on lid and place in oven
  9. Cook 325 degrees for four hours, or until tender and internal temp is 200
  10. With 70 minutes left, add other vegetables
